CipherKeys is a custom keyboard plus a "Decode with CipherKeys" share action.

How to test the keyboard:
1. Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → CipherKeys.
2. In any text field (e.g. Notes), type some text, tap the 🌐 globe to switch to CipherKeys, then tap Encode (and Decode to reverse).

Notes for review:
• The keyboard does NOT request Full Access (RequestsOpenAccess = NO) and works fully offline.
• No data is collected; there is no networking anywhere in the app or extensions.
• The ciphers are classic, reversible toys (Caesar, ROT13, Atbash, T9) — not encryption; the app makes no security/confidentiality claims.
• No account or login is required.
