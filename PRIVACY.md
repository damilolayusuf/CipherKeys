# CipherKeys Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-28_

CipherKeys is designed to collect **nothing**.

- **No data collection.** CipherKeys does not collect, store, or transmit any
  personal data, usage analytics, or the text you encode or decode.
- **No network access.** The app and its extensions perform all cipher
  transformations locally on your device. There is no networking code anywhere, so
  nothing leaves your device.
- **No accounts.** There is no sign-in, account, or profile.
- **Keyboard, no Full Access.** The CipherKeys keyboard runs with
  "Allow Full Access" turned **off**. It cannot send anything off your device and
  only transforms the text in the field you're editing when you tap Encode/Decode.
- **Settings storage.** Your selected cipher, Caesar shift, and saved recipes are
  stored only on your device (in a shared App Group container) so the app and the
  keyboard can use the same settings. This data never leaves your device.

## Important note

CipherKeys uses **classic, reversible ciphers** (Caesar, ROT13, Atbash, T9, and
user-built recipes). These are for fun and nostalgia — they are **not encryption**
and provide **no real security or confidentiality**. Anyone who has your cipher
recipe can read your message. Do not use CipherKeys to protect sensitive
information.
